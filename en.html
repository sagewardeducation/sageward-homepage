FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
 
Q  When is the best time to start planning?
The earlier, the more at ease you'll be. What matters is leaving time for activities and essay refinement, rather than rushing once application season arrives. Every student starts at a different time — the key is to start learning early and prepare step by step.
Q  What do your planning services include?
Academic and activity planning, college selection, essay guidance, interview preparation, and submission follow-through, with full support across the school year — or individual services chosen as needed.
Q  Do you write the essays for students?
No. Essays must come from the student. The advisor inspires ideas, helps shape the story, and refines the draft — but never writes on the student's behalf.
Q  Can you guarantee admission?
No. No organization can guarantee admission outcomes. What we can guarantee is professional planning and full commitment throughout — helping each student present their very best.
Q  How does high school planning differ from college planning?
High school planning centers on school fit and interview preparation; college planning places more weight on long-term activities and the essay narrative. Both need to start early — they simply differ in pace and emphasis.
Q  Is the first consultation free?
The first 30 minutes are free. We'll start by talking about your child's goals and where they stand, then decide together whether to work together.
